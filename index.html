About Us

Mathematics, made accessible for every learner

We are a grassroots mathematics education initiative working to close the gap between learners who have access to quality maths support and those who do not. Through tutoring, community engagement, and digital content, we meet learners where they are.

"Every learner who falls behind in mathematics is not failing maths—maths is failing them. We exist to change that." Make Maths Make Sense · Our founding belief
🎯

Targeted at underserved communities

We focus on learners in township, rural, and under-resourced schools who lack access to specialist maths support.

🌍

Real-world connections

We show learners that maths is alive in their communities—in markets, music, architecture, and technology.

🤝

Community-driven

Local volunteers, teachers, and mentors are at the heart of everything we do. Change comes from within the community.

Our Programmes

Five ways we make maths make sense

From one-on-one tutoring to digital content that travels further than any classroom, our programmes meet learners at every stage.

📚

Tutoring & Homework Support

Volunteer tutors provide after-school and weekend sessions for Grades 8–12. Small group sessions focus on building genuine understanding, not just exam technique.

🎥

Digital Content & Social Media

Short-form videos and carousel explainers break down trigonometry, calculus, and algebra into digestible, shareable lessons—on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.

🏫

In-School Workshops

We partner with schools to run intensive workshops before major assessments, helping learners tackle past papers with confidence and clarity.

🏆

Scholarships & Recognition

Promising learners from low-income backgrounds who demonstrate effort and growth are supported with scholarships for university maths and science programmes.

👩‍🏫

Teacher Development

We run capacity-building sessions for maths educators, sharing engaging teaching strategies, curriculum resources, and digital tools that bring the subject to life.

How We Work

A learner-centred methodology

We combine rigour with empathy. Every programme is designed around a learner's actual experience of mathematics—not a standardised curriculum checklist.

01

Diagnose

Identify exactly where a learner has gaps using diagnostic assessments—not assumptions. We start from the learner's actual baseline, not the curriculum's expected one.

02

Connect

Link every concept to a real-world context the learner recognises. Gradient becomes the slope of a township street. Probability becomes a cricket match or a taxi rank.

03

Practice

Build fluency through spaced, deliberate practice. We use worked examples, peer explanation, and digital exercises that give immediate, encouraging feedback.

04

Sustain

Track progress, celebrate growth, and adapt. We measure what matters—learner confidence and willingness to tackle hard problems, not just exam scores.
